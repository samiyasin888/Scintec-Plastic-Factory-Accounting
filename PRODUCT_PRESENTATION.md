## English Version

### Global Accounting
#### Professional Accounting and Inventory Management System

**Global Accounting** is a comprehensive desktop accounting and inventory management system designed for manufacturing and commercial businesses. The application provides a complete solution for managing invoices, inventory, payroll, reports, and company settings through an intuitive and user-friendly interface.

#### Key Features

- **Invoice Management** – Create, manage, and export sales invoices with automatic tax calculation
- **Inventory Tracking** – Monitor products, stock levels, and pricing with automatic quantity updates
- **Payroll Management** – Calculate employee salaries, bonuses, and deductions efficiently
- **Financial Reports** – Generate and export business reports and performance metrics
- **Company Settings** – Customize company name, country, currency, tax rate, and language
- **Bilingual Support** – Full support for Arabic and English languages
- **Local Database** – Secure SQLite database stored locally on the user's machine
- **Client Customization** – Fully customizable for each client's specific needs
- **Offline Operation** – Works completely offline without cloud dependency
- **Easy to Use** – Simple and clean interface designed for business users

#### Target Market

- Manufacturing factories
- Industrial companies
- Retail businesses
- Trading companies
- Small and medium-sized enterprises
- Any organization requiring daily invoice, inventory, and payroll management

#### Business Benefits

- **Time Saving** – Automate daily accounting and inventory tasks
- **Error Reduction** – Minimize manual data entry mistakes
- **Better Inventory Control** – Real-time tracking of stock and product information
- **Easy Invoice Management** – Quick creation and export of sales invoices
- **Efficient Payroll** – Simplified salary and employee management
- **Data-Driven Decisions** – Comprehensive reports for informed business decisions
- **Privacy & Security** – All data remains local and under client control
- **Scalability** – System grows with your business needs

#### System Requirements

- **Operating System:** Windows 7 SP1 or later
- **RAM:** 256 MB minimum
- **Disk Space:** 100 MB
- **Display:** 1024x768 resolution
- **Internet:** Not required (fully offline)

#### Installation

The application is available as:
1. **Windows Installer** – Professional installation with desktop shortcuts
2. **Portable Executable** – Run from any location without installation
3. **From Source** – Deploy using Python and provided scripts

#### Default Configuration

- **Company Name:** Scintec Plastic Factory
- **Country:** Saudi Arabia
- **Currency:** USD
- **Tax Rate:** 15%
- **Language:** English

All settings are fully customizable by the user.

#### Deployment

Each client receives:
- Individual installation on their machine
- Separate, isolated database in user AppData folder
- Full customization of company information
- No shared servers or external dependencies
- Complete data ownership and control

#### Technical Architecture

- **Frontend:** Tkinter (Python GUI framework)
- **Backend:** SQLite local database
- **Deployment:** PyInstaller (Windows executable)
- **Data Storage:** C:\Users\[username]\AppData\Local\GlobalAccounting\global.db
- **Architecture:** Standalone desktop application

#### المميزات الرئيسية

- **إدارة الفواتير** – إنشاء وإدارة وتصدير الفواتير مع حساب الضريبة التلقائي
- **متابعة المخزون** – مراقبة المنتجات والكميات والأسعار مع تحديث كمي تلقائي
- **إدارة الرواتب** – حساب رواتب الموظفين والبدلات والخصومات بكفاءة
- **التقارير المالية** – إنشاء وتصدير التقارير ومؤشرات الأداء
- **إعدادات الشركة** – تخصيص اسم الشركة والدولة والعملة ورسبة الضريبة واللغة
- **دعم ثنائي اللغة** – دعم كامل للعربية والإنجليزية
- **قاعدة بيانات محلية** – قاعدة بيانات SQLite آمنة تُحفظ محلياً على جهاز المستخدم
- **قابل للتخصيص** – قابل للتخصيص بالكامل حسب احتياجات كل عميل
- **يعمل دون اتصال إنترنت** – يعمل بشكل كامل دون الحاجة للسحابة
